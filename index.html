SINCE 1975

ABOUT NSS

The National Service Scheme is a student-oriented community service programme connected with higher education. At Raha College, NSS has carried this mission forward for five decades under Gauhati University's NSS Cell.

1975

NSS begins at Raha College.

1975–1999

Prof. Durlav Chandra Saikia serves as Programme Officer.

1999–2013

Prof. Mrinal Jyoti Sarma serves as Programme Officer.

2013–2016

Prof. Uttam Das serves as Programme Officer.

2016–2021

Prof. Biraj Hazarika serves as Programme Officer.

2021–Present

Prof. Parag Das serves as Programme Officer.

THE INSTITUTION

RAHA COLLEGE

A government, co-educational college at Raha, Nagaon, Assam – 782103, affiliated to Gauhati University. Raha College has long served students from Raha and the surrounding rural and socially disadvantaged communities through higher education.
